Invoice Lifecycle

How a freight invoice actually moves through CargoDesk — from an accrued cost line to cash received — including every gate it has to clear, every place the system reads or writes data, and the two ways it can end.

Scope. This traces customer-facing invoicing (accounts receivable) — the FR01/FR02 freight invoice and its CN01 credit/debit note. Freight Audit & Payment, the mirror-image system for reconciling what carriers bill CargoDesk, is a separate subsystem (routes/carrier-invoices.js) not shown here.

Data sources referenced

Every table, column set, or external service this flow reads from or writes to, in the order it first appears above.

SourceRole in this flow
shipment_cost_linesBUY/SELL charge lines. SELL lines are what an invoice is built from; a Reverse writes new negative, already-posted SELL lines back into this same table.
contract_rates · quote_linesWhere a cost line's figure usually originates from — a contract's live rate, or a quote's own independently-priced line.
charge_code_definitionsAdmin-maintained automated charges (e.g. a per-container fee) auto-injected only when generating a split-per-container invoice.
customerscredit_hold/credit_hold_reason, credit_limit/currency, credit_terms_days, and invoice_deadline_days — the four fields the credit gate and the deadline monitor both read.
contracts.named_account_idOn a Central contract, the credit hold check also covers the contract's own Named Account, not just the shipment's Shipper/Consignee/Principal.
shipment_documentsThe invoice record itself — status, confirmed_at (the AR-aging anchor), source_cost_line_ids, first_sent_at, paid_at/paid_amount, related_doc_id (links a CN01 back to what it reversed).
credit_overridesOne-time approvals from a shipment's own lane trade_manager, each valid for a 60-minute grace window — the only way past an over-limit block.
PDF Render Service (:3003)Separate microservice — renders the client-built invoice HTML to a PDF via a real headless browser.
org_signing_certsThe org's self-signed key, kept out of the plaintext app-settings table on purpose — used for a genuine CAdES-detached PKCS#7 signature over every generated document.
office_mail_settingsPer-office SMTP config. Email always sends from the shipment's Export Managing Office specifically — no fallback to another office.
document-distribution service (:3002)Separate microservice owning EDI transmittals and HMAC-signed outbound webhooks — never touches the monolith's own database.
shipment_milestonesThe delivered step's completion date anchors the Invoice Deadline Monitor, independently of whether an invoice exists yet.
