Carrier Offices

Each carrier gets a profile with its own offices, and the port or country each office covers per role: who takes our booking, who handles the arrival, and who bills us. A shipment picks its carrier offices from its port of loading and port of discharge. Rows marked Real come from your database; rows marked Example are your Spain case, typed in to show the rules (your database has no Spanish office, entity or shipment).

The five points, settled on what DCSA, CargoWise and other systems have in common

    
You asked me to check each point against the DCSA standard, CargoWise and other systems, and to go with what they have in common.
      A to D held. C and E changed slightly: no separate agents list, and offices are picked again only when the carrier or a port changes.

    

      
A
No billing office covers the port. The booking office bills on the export side and the import office on the import side.
        This is worked out each time it's read, so a billing row added later takes over.
        SAP: no invoicing party means the order's vendor. Oracle: no alternate pay site means the order's site. CargoWise: no payable related party
        means the organisation itself. DCSA: "payable at" is optional on a booking and there is no billing party.

      
B
Who issued a carrier invoice. One "Issued by" per invoice, filled from the shipment's billing office for the side of its
        first charge. The clerk can change it until a line is approved; after that it's fixed.
        CargoWise: the creditor is on the AP invoice header. SAP: one invoicing party per invoice, fixed once posted. Oracle: one supplier site
        per invoice, defaulted from the order and changeable. Magaya: the vendor is picked per bill, its address filled in and changeable for that bill. DCSA has no
        invoice standard.

      
C
Where the profile lives. On the carrier's own record: Master Data → Carriers → the carrier. The separate Carrier Agents
        list goes, and its old link opens Carriers.
        CargoWise keeps the addresses and appointed-agent ports on the carrier's organisation, SAP the partner functions on the vendor, Oracle the
        sites on the supplier, and Magaya the carrier entity. None has a separate agents list.

      
D
The creditor code. One per carrier office.
        In all four, the creditor is the company that bills: the CargoWise organisation, the SAP vendor, the Oracle supplier and pay site, the
        Magaya vendor. Only CargoWise adds an outside code per company of ours, so it's left out.

      
E
Shipments that already exist. Each shipment keeps a copy of its offices, and a change in Master Data never rewrites it.
        The offices are picked again only when the shipment's carrier or one of its ports changes. Today's Line Agents move over as the shipment's booking and
        import offices, with their names kept. Nothing is resent to the carrier.
        SAP: orders made before an invoicing party was added don't get it. Oracle: a site change affects new documents only. CargoWise: a
        port's agent is filled in when the port is entered. DCSA: changing a booking already sent is an amendment, never automatic.
