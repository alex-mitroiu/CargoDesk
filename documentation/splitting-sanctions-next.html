Splitting Sanctions Next

A full pass over all ~90 tables to answer one question directly: after MDM, what's the next clean cut? Kanban isn't it — its tickets table reaches into shipments, users, and itself. Sanctions/screening is — a denylist synced from two external feeds, read by name-match, with zero outbound foreign key anywhere.

How screening works today

Two external lists feed one table. Nothing about how it's read touches SQL on the request path at all — screening never queries sanctions_entries directly, it matches against an in-memory index built once at boot and rebuilt after every sync.

Sanctions vs. Kanban — why one clears the bar

Same question the MDM-vs-Users diagram asked, aimed at the two live candidates from this thread. Sanctions points at nothing; Kanban points at three different domains, one of which writes back into it unprompted.

The proposed cut

Same shape as MDM's own proposal, one more time: the monolith keeps a local fallback, a new Screening Service owns the real data and the two sync jobs, and a screening_source toggle switches between them without a deploy.

Sequencing

MDM and Sanctions are both low-risk, both still just proposals — they can ship back to back, ahead of the two domains that actually need more runway.
