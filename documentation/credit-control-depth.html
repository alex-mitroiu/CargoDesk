CargoDesk today

Read directly from routes/customers.js and src/utils/invoiceGenerator.js — not from memory or documentation, which had drifted.

Data model

customers.credit_limit (nullable, USD only — the Profile tab literally labels the field "Credit Limit (USD, optional)"), credit_terms_days (stored on every customer, but not read by any other code path in the app), credit_hold / credit_hold_reason.

The one trigger point

resolveCreditGate() runs exclusively when a user clicks Generate Invoice on the Accounting → Invoice Entry page. Nothing checks credit at shipment creation, carrier booking, or quote stage — by the time the gate fires, the shipment has typically already been fully executed.

What the gate actually does

Credit hold — a hard block, no override in the UI, checked against whichever of Shipper / Consignee / Principal / the linked contract's Named Account is on hold.
Credit limit — a soft warning only: outstanding AR (confirmed, non-voided FR01/FR02 invoices billed to Principal-then-Consignee, resolved to the cent via source_cost_line_ids) plus the invoice about to be generated, compared to the limit. "Cancel" or "Generate Anyway" — available to anyone who can generate invoices at all, no separate permission tier.

What's absent

AR aging buckets, accrued-but-not-yet-invoiced exposure, a parent/group credit-limit rollup (despite parent_customer_id + resolveCustomerGroup() already existing for margin reporting since v0.59.0), a role gate on who can set credit fields or override the warning, dunning/statement automation, credit-insurance or bad-debt tracking, and any formal limit-request/approval workflow.

CargoWise One — confirmed vs. unconfirmed

WiseTech publishes no technical/data-model documentation for CW1 — every claim below is sourced to a specific cargowise.com product page or news post, not inferred.

confirmed

Credit is configured on the Organization (customer) record itself — "your customers and suppliers have their own individual accounting configuration for both receivables and payables... you can also set up client invoicing preferences – and manage their credit – directly within this organisation record." Matches CargoDesk's existing per-customer model.

confirmed

The credit check fires at shipment creation, not only at invoicing: "Evaluate customer balance and credit status during shipment creation, giving your collections team the leverage to collect without disrupting business from key customers." — the single most load-bearing finding in this whole analysis.

confirmed

Access to run a credit check or set a limit can be restricted to a specific department — "you can also easily set accounting as the only department that can run a credit check or set a credit limit," under a "Configure powerful security settings" heading.

confirmed

CW1 generates real WIP/accrual GL transactions for job revenue that's operationally recognized but not yet invoiced (a 2022 release note). Whether that figure specifically feeds the credit-check exposure calculation is not confirmed — noted honestly rather than assumed.

confirmed

A distinct "CargoWise Credit Reports" add-on integrates illion and Dun & Bradstreet commercial credit-bureau data for external risk appraisal of new and existing customers — a genuinely different capability from internal AR tracking, and one CargoDesk has no equivalent of.

unconfirmed

Per-currency vs. base-currency limit representation; a parent/group credit-limit hierarchy; whether the trigger point is configurable to quote or booking stage; whether a credit hold is a true hard block and what role can override it; whether AR aging buckets (vs. a flat total) drive the calculation; any dunning/statement automation; any bad-debt or credit-insurance tracking; any formal limit-approval workflow. No sourced CW1 material was found for any of these — they are not asserted as fact anywhere in this document.

Magaya Supply Chain — confirmed vs. unconfirmed

Sourced to the official Magaya Cargo System User Manual, Part II: Accounting — a real primary-source PDF with screenshots of the actual product. Dated 2009, pre-dating the 2023 Descartes acquisition; current-version confirmation wasn't reachable (help.magaya.com is a JS-rendered SPA that returned an empty shell to fetch tools).

confirmed

When a customer is over the limit, the system can independently enforce any combination of three gates: "Do not receive more cargo," "Do not accept more bookings (online also)," "Do not release the cargo." Three distinct, earlier, more granular lifecycle stops than CargoDesk's single invoice-time check.

confirmed

A real Aging A/R report — Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, >90 days, dollar totals per bucket per customer. Genuinely bucketed, not a flat total.

confirmed

Overpayments are tracked as customer credits (unapplied cash), usable against future invoices or refundable — implies a real payment/cash-application subledger CargoDesk doesn't have at all.

unconfirmed

Whether the credit-limit check itself nets out unapplied credits or accounts for committed-but-unbilled exposure — the manual's documented trigger conditions describe AR (invoiced) balances only.

not documented

No override/bypass role for the hard block; no dunning or statement-of-account automation; no credit-insurance or bad-debt provisioning; no formal credit-approval workflow — none of these appear anywhere in the 106-page manual.

The six stories

All logged under Epic TKT-6XFJQM, status Ready. Ordered by the ranking above.

AR aging buckets on credit-statusTKT-O4DNFX

Extend GET /api/customers/:id/credit-status with Current/1-30/31-60/61-90/90+ buckets, computed from each confirmed invoice's date plus the customer's own credit_terms_days. Read-side aggregation only — no schema change. Surfaces on the Profile Billing tab and inside the existing invoice-generation gate warning.

Count accrued-but-uninvoiced exposureTKT-AJAEDO

Add a separate committedExposure figure — accrued/actualized SELL cost lines not yet invoiced — kept visibly distinct from real invoiced AR, never silently merged into one number.

Earlier trigger pointsTKT-Q00WHF

A soft, informational check at shipment creation (parties often aren't all known yet), and a real blocking gate at carrier-booking send time — reusing CarrierBookingGateModal's already-established blocking pattern rather than inventing a new one.

Parent/group credit rollupTKT-IA7I7J

Wire the existing resolveCustomerGroup() helper into credit-status as an additive groupOutstandingAr, mirroring the non-breaking pattern the v0.59.0 margin-rollup toggle already used.

Trade-lane-scoped credit override, exclusive to that lane's trade managerTKT-GLWMFP

Explicit rule, not inferred: override authority belongs only to the trade_manager responsible for that shipment's own trade lane — never admin, operator, or a trade manager outside it. No fallback bypass for any other role, matching CW1's own confirmed department-exclusive precedent. trade_manager keeps its existing Accounting-wide hide (v0.29.0) — this is a narrow carve-out, not a reopening. Reuses user_scope_items's already-working item_type='trade_lane' (matched via the same portLanesMap infrastructure applyShipmentAccessFilter already uses to scope a user's own shipment visibility) rather than inventing lane-ownership from scratch. A new, small surface outside Accounting lists credit-blocked/over-limit shipments scoped to the viewing manager's own lane(s); releasing there is step one, the actual invoice generation by operator/admin is step two — trade_manager still has no invoicing UI of its own. Server-side enforcement in both resolveCreditGate and the customer PUT route, not just hidden client-side.

Overdue-invoice remindersTKT-SUEDWH

Lower priority, deliberately not claimed as a competitive gap. Reuses the existing per-office SMTP mailer once AR aging (story one) exists to trigger off of.

Country-defaulted customer currency, wired into credit-limit interpretationTKT-O5I4NK

Direct follow-up request: a Spain-based customer's credit limit should default to EUR, not hardcoded USD, with conversion done at the shipment level. customers.currency already exists (used today only to pick a multi-currency invoice's grand-total display currency) — this extends its purpose rather than adding a second field. A small static ISO2→currency lookup (mirrors the existing COUNTRY_TZ map's own precedent) auto-fills it at customer-create time, still a real dropdown constrained to the existing currency list, always overridable. Conversion reuses the exact toUsd/FX machinery every cost line already uses — no new FX system. Data-safety note: every existing credit_limit was entered assuming USD; a one-time backfill locks currency='USD' on all existing customers first, so nothing already stored is silently reinterpreted — only new customers pick up the country default.

Explicitly out of scope

Named directly rather than silently dropped — the same discipline the original competitive gap analysis applied to carrier networks and market-rate indices.

Not pursued

  • Credit-bureau / external risk-reference integration. CW1's illion/Dun & Bradstreet partnership needs a paid data-vendor relationship — the same structurally-out-of-reach class as the original sweep's carrier-network and market-index findings. No amount of engineering closes this alone.
  • Trade credit insurance / bad-debt provisioning. Neither researched platform confirmed this as a real, documented feature — not a proven gap.
  • A full payment/cash-application subledger. Magaya's "unapplied cash as customer credit" implies a genuine AR receipts module CargoDesk has no equivalent of at all. That's a separate, materially larger epic in its own right — a real receivables ledger, not a credit-control tweak — and shouldn't be scoped into this pass.
