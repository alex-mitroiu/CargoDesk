Splitting MDM First

Following up on the DB-split conversation: MDM (carriers, vessels, ports, trade lanes, commodities) is the simpler of the two candidates to extract first, and it follows a mechanism CargoDesk has already proven once. Users/Auth stays put — it's the harder cut, not the first one.

Where we are today

This isn't CargoDesk's first extraction. Three services already run standalone; Contract Management is the one that matters here, since it already coexists with local tables behind a toggle — exactly the shape an MDM split would reuse.

MDM vs. Users — why one is simpler

Same question, drawn twice. MDM is read by a handful of named lookup functions and written to almost nowhere outside admin/import tooling. Users sits in front of every single request and is joined into features that have nothing to do with authentication.

The proposed cut

Same shape as Contract Management, renamed: a new MDM Service with its own database, a callMdmService() helper, and an mdm_source toggle so the switch is reversible in a config change, not a deploy.

Sequencing

MDM first, then the already-logged Customer/Organization epic, then Shipments last — it's the core of the app and the highest blast radius, so it earns the most runway before it moves.

Draft for discussion — nothing here is built yet. Based on CargoDesk v0.78.0's actual table layout and the Contract Management extraction (v0.68.0) as precedent.
