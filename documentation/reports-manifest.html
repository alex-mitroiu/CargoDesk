01 gp-trend
Recommended✓ Shipped

Period-over-period comparison

Show each region/country/carrier row's GP and margin delta against the immediately preceding period of the same length — e.g. ▲ 4.2 pts vs prior 30 days. Reuses the exact same aggregation query the report already runs, just executed twice over two adjacent windows instead of once.

Why this one

A snapshot alone can't say whether a below-target lane is getting worse or already recovering — and that direction is the real question behind "investigate why GP is below target."

Tradeoff

Roughly doubles the DB work per request (two aggregations instead of one), and needs a defensible "previous period" when either end of the date range is left open — there's no natural prior window to diff against.

02 gp-digest
Alternative~ Partially fulfilled

Scheduled email digest

A recurring send (e.g. weekly) of this same report to whoever owns a region or carrier lane, rendered the same way the live report already does.

Why this one

Serves ongoing monitoring rather than ad-hoc investigation — useful once someone's job is to watch a lane, not just check in on demand.

Tradeoff

Mostly wiring, not new plumbing — per-office SMTP config and compose infrastructure already shipped for document distribution. But it solves a distribution problem, not an analysis one, so it's only worth building if periodic monitoring is the actual need.
