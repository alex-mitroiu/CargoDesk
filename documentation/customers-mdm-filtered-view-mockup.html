Same role vocabulary as before — "Trading Customers" groups Shipper/Consignee/ Principal/Notify Party/Also Notify Party (who CargoDesk bills or acts for); "Service Providers" groups Forwarder/Customs Broker/Trucker/Bank/Insurance/Agent (who CargoDesk pays or coordinates with). A company used as both would simply show role badges from both groups and appear under "All" — nothing forces a company into only one bucket. CFS and Terminal aren't modeled as roles yet — if you want those trackable the same way, that's a small addition to the existing role list (not a new table), separate from this filtering decision.

Status update, verified 2026-08-25: the role vocabulary was 13 (4 fixed + 9 additional) when this mockup was built — it's grown to 16 (4 fixed + 12 additional) since, adding Line Agent (Export)/ Line Agent (Import) (v0.61.0) and NVOCC (v0.71.0) to the Service Providers group, now 11 roles there not 6/8. Also: the real shipped table has 7 columns, not 5 — Phone and Website are shown alongside Company/Roles/City-Country/Email/Actions.
