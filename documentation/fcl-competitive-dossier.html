01

Bottom line

CargoDesk's FCL workflow — quoting through invoicing — is more complete than a solo-built platform has any right to be. Every stage a working FCL desk touches daily is a real, stateful, validated workflow, not a stub. The gap that actually matters isn't feature breadth — it's who's on the other end of the wire.

1

Zero live carriers, despite excellent plumbing

CargoDesk has real, spec-accurate DCSA Booking API integration — an adapter registry, a signed webhook receiver, a Booking v2.0.5 adapter built against the actual public standard. But no carrier is actually signed up and connected today. Every booking, SI, VGM, and customs-filing exchange in production runs through an internal simulator.

This is a business step (sign one carrier), not a rebuild — the engineering is already sitting there waiting for a sandbox.

2

Freight Audit and export-compliance depth are real strengths

These are the two areas where CargoDesk's engineering is at or above what mid-market competitor Magaya documents publicly, and conceptually matches CargoWise's own described AI three-way match — unglamorous, correctness-critical work that experienced ops people specifically look for.

3

Visibility is real at the vessel level only

A live AIS feed genuinely auto-confirms vessel departure/arrival. But every container-level and terminal event — gate in/out, discharge — is still hand-typed. This is exactly the gap project44 and FourKites charge a premium to close, and it's the most visible difference if someone compares a CargoDesk shipment screen to a project44-fed dashboard side by side.

03

Where CargoDesk leads or matches

Not just a gap list — six places the evidence supports parity with, or an edge over, named competitors.

Freight Audit matching depth

Independent DET/DEM free-time windows and a safeguard against silently overwriting an already-posted cost line — conceptually matches CargoWise's own described AI three-way match, and appears to exceed what Magaya documents publicly.

AES/EEI export-control depth

ECCN, Schedule B, license type/value per cargo line, plus a live staleness check if the shipment changes after filing — comparable to Descartes' dedicated compliance product line, built as one feature inside a general TMS.

VGM's full SOLAS cycle

Method 1/Method 2 declaration with a real submit/accept/reject cycle and milestone auto-completion, matching what CargoWise and Magaya describe as standard.

Quote-to-shipment conversion

One atomic transaction carries over parties, derives containers, creates SELL and BUY cost lines, checks credit hold, screens for denied parties, and recomputes the space badge — deeper than what Magaya's public materials describe for the same handoff.

The AIS integration is real, not vaporware

A live third-party feed doing genuine geofence-based departure/arrival detection — the same underlying technique project44 uses, at a fraction of the coverage. Worth saying plainly so it isn't lumped in with "everything is simulated."

Multi-entity accounting

FX-aware margin-by-entity reporting with branch scoping is enterprise-grade, and likely ahead of Magaya's more SMB-oriented accounting.

04

The real gaps, classified

Split the way the last competitive pass split its findings: work that's genuinely engineering, and work that no amount of engineering closes alone.

Buildable

Real engineering effort, bounded and scoped
  • DCSA Track & Trace event ingestionThe booking half is done; a webhook/polling handler for gate moves and discharge slots into the existing milestone-completion pattern.
  • Validate the DCSA adapter against a real carrier sandboxMostly a signup step (Hapag-Lloyd's sandbox is public self-service), with a modest engineering tail to fix real-spec deviations.
  • Freight Audit matching-engine breadthMore charge codes and D&D edge cases — incremental extension of an engine that already works.
  • Direct AES/EEI government EDI transmissionA defined, documented API (AESDirect/ACE); needs filer credentials from the business, then a bounded build following the DCSA adapter's own pattern.

Structural — not a code gap

Years of partnerships or a data business, not a sprint
  • CargoWise/Descartes-scale carrier network breadth20+ years of bilateral EDI certifications with hundreds of carriers. A network-effects moat, not a backlog item.
  • project44/FourKites-style global visibility98%+ ocean freight, 700+ ports — needs commercial data deals with terminals and carriers worldwide.
  • CargoSphere/Freightos-style rate benchmarkingThe product exists only because it pools real transaction data across thousands of forwarders. A single-tenant platform has no comparable pool.
  • Legally negotiable eB/LNeeds accreditation with a title registry banks and customs trust as authoritative — a legal/network problem, not a coding one.
05

Detailed evidence

Full citations behind every matrix row — collapsed by default, open any that need checking.

Quote → booking conversion Full

routes/quotes.js:1-52 — real Draft→Sent→Accepted/Declined/Expired→Converted lifecycle with an hourly auto-expire sweep. Conversion (:329-484) is atomic: carries shipper/consignee/principal/notify parties, derives containers from quote lines (:391-426), creates SELL cost lines from the agreed price (:380-389) and BUY lines from the matched contract (:378), auto-assigns carrier line agents (:377), recomputes the space/TEU badge (:433), runs denied-party screening (:435), and surfaces a credit-hold warning across all three carried-over parties (:441-446).

Carrier booking workflow Full workflow0 live

routes/edi.js carries carrier_bookings.status through Pending→Confirmed/Rejected/Cancelled (:140,149,449-459,508-515), generating BR01/BC01 documents (src/utils/documentBuilders.js:11-14,370,460). The DCSA Booking API v2.0.5 adapter (lib/carrier-integrations/dcsa-bkg-v2.js) is built against the real public spec and is carrier-agnostic — any DCSA member could plug in via one config row (:5-8). A signed webhook receiver exists (routes/carrier-webhooks.js, HMAC-SHA256, master kill switch). The callback-signing scheme is explicitly disclosed as a placeholder pending a real sandbox (dcsa-bkg-v2.js:118-121). A real, live Maersk Booking API integration existed and was removed in v0.72.0 when Maersk's developer-tools dependency went obsolete (routes/edi.js:34-36). Today, booking confirmation/rejection in production runs exclusively through the Test Tools Message Simulator (routes/edi.js:32-87).

Schedules & routing Full

routes/system.js has a real internal schedule catalog (catalogSailings, :349-402, with transit days and leg breakdowns) and a mock-data fallback (mockSailings, :301-348). A leg-cascade Cypress spec (cypress/e2e/schedule-leg-cascade.cy.js) confirms multi-leg transshipment is real and tested. dcsa-bkg-v2.js:85-116 adds a DCSA schedule search, flagged in its own comment as best-effort pending a real sandbox.

VGM — SOLAS Method 1/2 Full

Pending→Submitted is blocked without a declared Method 1 (Weighing) or Method 2 (Calculated) (routes/shipments.js:1009-1021; UI at src/pages/shipments/VgmServicePage.jsx:45-46,82-83) →Accepted/Rejected via a dedicated simulator (:1132-1168), with a vgm_declaration EDI message logged on submit (:1111-1122, explicitly simulated) and automatic milestone completion once every container is Submitted (:1123-1126).

Shipping Instructions Full, simulated

routes/shipping-instructions.js implements an independent Draft→Submitted→Confirmed/Rejected machine (mirrors customs-filing's shape, :1-9), gated on shipper/consignee/container completeness (:62-65), auto-completing the si_submitted milestone (:81), with reset-and-resubmit after rejection (:116-127). Explicitly disclosed as simulated, no live carrier EDI (:8).

AES/EEI & ISF/AMS filing Full

routes/customs-filing.js supports both AES/EEI (export) and ISF/AMS (import) as independent filing types per shipment (UNIQUE(shipment_id, filing_type), :1-12), each with its own Draft→Filed→Accepted/Rejected cycle, required-broker-role gate (:117-119), priced-cargo gate, and USPPI/Ultimate-Consignee validation (:126-130). Export-control fields (Schedule B, ECCN, license type/value) come from container_packages (:39-53) and are snapshotted at filing time. A live staleness check recomputes whether the snapshot still matches the shipment's current carrier/vessel/voyage/cargo (:56-70). ISF/AMS is confirmed present as filing type ISF_AMS — easy to miss on a bare name search. Simulated only, no live AESDirect/ACE transmission (:11-12).

House B/L lifecycle Partial

BL01 auto-completes the bl_issued milestone (routes/shipment-ops.js:1263; regression-tested in tests/house-bl-lifecycle.test.js:118-140, including that a manually-completed milestone is never overwritten). Mark Surrendered / Mark Released are idempotent and logged as entity events (tests/house-bl-lifecycle.test.js:142-162). A bl_release_type field distinguishes Original/Telex Release/Surrendered/Seaway Bill (routes/shipments.js:77). No electronic B/L transfer (Bolero/essDOCS/WaveBL-style) exists anywhere in the codebase.

Milestones & real-time tracking Partial

The FCL template has 9 steps (routes/shipment-ops.js:963-971). vessel_departed/vessel_arrived are genuinely live — a persistent WebSocket to aisstream.io (lib/ais-listener.js:1-52) confirms them by matching vessel position and navigational status against the leg's POL/POD via haversine proximity (:42-50). cargo_gated_in/cargo_released auto-complete only once every container has a manually-logged Gate In/Out event (routes/shipments.js:1462-1491) — 100% hand-entered. si_submitted, customs_cleared, booking_confirmed auto-complete from CargoDesk's own internal state changes, fed by simulated upstream events. bl_issued is an internal document-confirm trigger. delivered has no auto-complete path at all — manual only. Despite its name, the DCSA adapter implements booking and schedule search only — no Track & Trace event ingestion exists.

Freight Audit & Payment Full

routes/carrier-invoices.js reconciles a carrier's invoice line against an already-accrued cost line where one exists (:128-143) or the live contract rate otherwise (:145-154). Detention and Demurrage are distinct charge types with independent free-time windows and independent container_events anchor pairs (:91-125) — a previously-fixed bug is noted in the code where both used to share one window (:96-98). A configurable variance-tolerance percentage (:170) drives matched/variance classification; an exception queue surfaces worst-variance-first, capped at 200 rows (:200-226). Approving a line actualizes the matched cost line or creates a new one, and refuses to silently overwrite an already-posted line (:401-417). Full dispute capture and audit-event logging throughout.

Invoicing, credit notes & multi-entity accounting Full

FR01 invoice and CN01 credit/debit note generation are both real (src/utils/invoiceGenerator.js:413,422,429,443,449), backed by an invoice-reversal test suite and an invoice-collections/billing-performance UI. Multi-entity/branch accounting is real: branch-level currency, FX-aware margin-by-entity resolved from the shipment's EMO/IMO office's branch, and branch-scoped visibility for non-global users (tests/multi-entity-accounting.test.js:1-8, routes/organization.js, src/pages/org/BranchPage.jsx).

Multi-carrier integration depth Plumbing0 live

The registry pattern, the spec-accurate DCSA adapter, and the signed webhook receiver are real working code (lib/carrier-integrations/registry.js, lib/carrier-integrations/dcsa-bkg-v2.js, routes/carrier-webhooks.js) — wired end-to-end to carrier_bookings/edi_messages and unit-tested (tests/carrier-integrations.test.js). But no carrier has been signed up and validated against it; the callback signature scheme is explicitly unverified, and the one carrier this was scoped against (Hapag-Lloyd, chosen for its public self-service sandbox) has not been onboarded — deliberately left to the business side.

Denied-party screening Full

Two syncable list sources: OFAC SDN and the Consolidated Screening List (routes/sanctions.js, parseOfacCsv, syncOfacSdn/syncConsolidatedScreeningList). Sync/import is admin-only and rate-limited after a fixed privilege-escalation bug (a viewer-role account could previously wipe the dataset, :24-35). A dedicated multi-list regression test exists (tests/multi-list-screening.test.js). Comparable in list coverage to Magaya's dedicated DPS product; CSL is a superset of most of what Magaya lists separately.
